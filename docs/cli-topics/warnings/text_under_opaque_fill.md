---
code: text_under_opaque_fill
availability: always on (operator-list signal; no `--geometry`, `--vector-boxes`, or `--annotations` flag required)
summary: Native text is still extractable under a later-drawn opaque dark rectangular fill, which can indicate an ineffective visual-only redaction.
---

It fires on native text of at least 3 non-whitespace characters hidden by a later-drawn rectangular path fill with alpha from 0.9 through 1, dark luminance at most 0.2, normal blend mode, and no active clip, soft mask, transparency group, or optional-content frame. It reuses the page operator list already fetched for image/vector analysis.

Coverage is decided **per glyph** wherever the operator list lets pdfvision place each glyph (it replays the text matrix, `Td`/`TJ` offsets, character/word spacing, horizontal scaling, rise, and the font matrix the way pdf.js paints them): a glyph is hidden when the fill covers at least 90% of its body (the middle of its advance, from just above the baseline to about x-height), so a box over a few words inside a sentence — a black box over a value, the usual shape of a failed redaction — is found. Consecutive hidden glyphs on one line form a passage; a glyph that later text is painted on top of (a white label over a dark bar) is not hidden. The rest of the line is visible text and reads normally — only the named passages are hidden.

The warning lists up to three passages as `"text" (bbox x,y,w,h)` in raw page units, ready for `--render-region`, plus a count; when there are more it adds the bbox enclosing all of them.

Runs whose glyphs cannot be placed (vertical writing, negative font size, missing advance widths) fall back to whole-span coverage of at least 90%, which reports the span text and bbox. For that fallback, detection aligns normalized spans from the end against ordered operator-list text-show runs; one span may match an exact deterministic concatenation of adjacent runs, and only fills painted after the last ordered exact match qualify. When extraction deduplicates identical text paints, that last ordered exact match decides whether the fill is actually later, which prevents a deduplicated span from being treated as hidden when identical foreground text was repainted after the fill.

**Limits:** the path data itself must prove positive geometry: a single closed axis-aligned rectangle under an axis-preserving CTM, encoded in at most 16 path numbers. Complex paths (including holes, even-odd frames with holes, and disconnected subpaths), annotation appearance operators, strokes, light/translucent/transparent, clipped, soft-masked, transparency-group, or optional-content fills, non-normal or unparseable blend-mode fills, underlines and strike-through rules (they cover too little of a glyph's body), boxes over fewer than 3 characters, fills painted before foreground text, rotated or skewed path boxes, invalid geometry, and unparseable colors are ignored. CTM, fill color/transparency, nonstroking alpha, blend mode, clip, soft-mask, and marked-content state are tracked through save/restore and Form XObjects; Form BBox clips are treated as active clips. Collection and matching fail closed when page evidence exceeds 4,096 candidate spans, text runs, or candidate fills; 65,536 span, raw-run, or NFKC-normalized UTF-16 code units; a shared budget of 1,000,000 run/code-unit comparison steps; or 250,000 span/fill coverage checks per page; the glyph path fails closed above 2,000,000 glyph/fill and glyph/glyph comparisons. Detection is suppressed on raster-backed OCR text layers. The warning does not change `quality`.

**Next step:** this is an error-level trust signal. A reader of the page sees a black box where `pages[].text` has the passage, so do not present the passage as visible content; whether to disclose it is the agent's call. Treat the listed passages and matching native search hits as exposed hidden content; `--render-region` on a listed bbox shows what a reader sees there.
