**Page 1 — 1 Executive summary.** The programme replaces diesel vans at our regional depots with battery-electric vehicles and installs the charging they need. This report covers the third quarter of 2026 and the position at 30 September. Overall the programme is on budget and four weeks behind the charger schedule set in January. Vehicle deliveries are ahead of plan, so the constraint for the rest of the year is charging capacity, not vehicles. Key points (numbered 1–4):

1. Chargers installed rose from 212 to 513 in the quarter, against a year-end target of 696.
2. Average charger utilisation reached 63%, above the 55% planning assumption.
3. Two depots remain blocked on grid upgrades; both have dates confirmed by the network operator.
4. Spend to date is EUR 18.4 million of the EUR 31.0 million budget.

The budget sentence carries footnote 1: “Budget figures exclude grid-connection fees, which the network operators invoice separately.”
