Getting started

1

REMOVE ALL PACKAGING
Take the limescale filter (3) out of the spout, rinse it and the inside of the kettle body (4) with clean water, then refit
the filter.

2

SET UP THE BASE

Place the power base (7) on a dry, level surface away from the edge of the worktop. Unwind only as much power
cord (8) as you need.

3

FILL WITH WATER

Press the lid release button (2) to open the lid (1). Fill to between MIN (0.5 L) and MAX (1.7 L) on the water level
window (5), then close the lid.

4

SWITCH ON

Seat the kettle on the base and press the power switch (6) down. The light comes on, and the kettle switches off
by itself when the water boils.

5

DISCARD THE FIRST TWO BOILS

Before first use, boil a full kettle and pour the water away. Repeat once more; after that the kettle is ready for
drinks.

WARNING — Risk of scalding

Never open the lid while the water is boiling or lift the kettle off the base mid-boil.
Do not fill above MAX: boiling water may be ejected from the spout.
