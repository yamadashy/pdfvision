Line 4a and 4b — IRA Distributions

Exception 1. Do not include in line 4b
any amount you rolled over to another
traditional IRA within 60 days, unless the
rollover was made after the deadline for
2026.

Exception 2. If you converted part or all
of a traditional IRA to a Roth IRA in 2025,
enter the amount converted on line 4a
and also report it on your own Form
8606.

Exception 3. A qualified charitable
distribution is not taxable. Enter QCD
next to line 4b and keep the statement
from the trustee with your records for
2026.

More than one exception. If more than
one exception applies, attach a
statement showing the amount of each
exception instead of making an entry
next to line 4b; see also your Form 5329.

Basis. If you made nondeductible
contributions to any IRA, your basis is
figured on the worksheet and the result is
carried to line 13 of your separate Form
8606.

Additional tax. You may have to pay an
additional tax if you received an early
distribution and the total is more than
your basis; see the instructions for Form
5329.

Required minimum. If you were born
before 1951 you must take a minimum
distribution each year; a shortfall in any
one year is reported in Part IX of Form
5329.

Disaster relief. Distributions made
because of a federally declared disaster
may be spread over three years; report
the first part on Form 8915-F filed for
2026.

Repayments. Amounts you repay within
three years are treated as a rollover and
reduce the taxable amount reported on
line 4b of your return for 2025 and for
2026.
